Notch size: Standard or Large

Settings › General › Notch size. Each size is one fixed open size for every tab (D16, D29 still hold); it changes only from Settings. Standard is today's 596 × 190 pt on the Air (six top-row items). Choose Large's extra height and width below; each tab is drawn at Standard, then at Large. Large also takes one text step up, so the artwork, type, calendar rows and controls grow with the space; switch that off to see more space with today's type.

How each tab uses the space

  • Now Playing: the artwork takes the height the bar row leaves; title, artist, time and controls one step larger.
  • Calendar: 40 % of the row as now; weekday and date larger, rows of 16 + 2 pt instead of 14 + 2.
  • Clipboard: a taller search field and 28 pt rows at 13 pt text.
  • Terminal: more rows and columns at your Settings › Terminal font size (11 pt here); the font doesn't change with the notch size.
  • Notes and Tray (not drawn): Notes gets more lines at a larger size; the tray's tiles could grow to 84 × 78 pt.

Not in the picture

  • Large widens past what the tabs need; with fewer tabs on, Standard stays 536 pt and Large adds the same amount to it.
  • A larger open notch covers more of the window under it while open; hover-out (D18) is unchanged.
  • 14-inch MacBook Pro and Larger Text: checked on a device after the pick.

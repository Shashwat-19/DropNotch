Terminal tab at 190 pt (MacBook Air)

The open notch with a Terminal tab, at the fixed 190 pt height. The terminal uses the same margins as every other tab (D37). Layout A puts the session controls in the empty strip left of the camera housing, so the terminal keeps every row. Layout B puts them in a footer under the terminal instead. Yellow figures are measured; the grid shows the cells.

Rows × columns at 190 pt

  • Six top-row items (Home, Notes, Tray, Clipboard, Terminal and Settings) make the notch 596 pt wide; with Clipboard off it stays 536 pt (D45 Q5).
  • Not focused: the cursor is hollow and keys go to the app in front. Focused (after a click in the terminal or ⌃⌥N): the cursor is solid, and the notch stays open when the pointer leaves.
  • Layout A's strip left of the housing is 132 pt at 536 pt and 162 pt at 596 pt: room for the shell's name and End Session, not the size, which goes in the button's tooltip.
  • Ended: the shell exited or End Session was pressed; nothing runs until Start New Session.

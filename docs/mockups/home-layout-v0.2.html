Per tab

  • Now Playing: the artwork grows by the full height gain; elapsed and total time return under the bar at every option.
  • Calendar: weekday over the date, then rows of 14 pt + 2 pt. Rows only step every 16 pt, so some heights add no row.
  • Clipboard: search, then 24 pt rows; a cut-off row hints that the list scrolls.
  • Notes: more lines of the editor.
  • Tray: the tiles and actions take 95 pt and don't grow, so the extra height is empty unless the tiles get bigger (not in this branch).
